ZeroSettle

ZeroSettle is an iMessage API for businesses and AI agents. Pro costs $49/month for one dedicated iMessage line on Apple hardware we manage.

You can send iMessages with a REST API, receive replies through signed webhooks, or connect an AI agent through MCP (Model Context Protocol). Teams use ZeroSettle for customer support, retention offers to opted-in users, and sign-in by text.

Pro is self-serve. Growth is invite-only for teams that need multiple lines. Enterprise has custom pricing for higher volume and compliance needs.
