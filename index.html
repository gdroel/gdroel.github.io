Gabe Roeloffs

Technology entrepreneur and angel investor

Gabe Roeloffs is a technology entrepreneur, angel investor, and co-founder and CEO of ZeroSettle, an iMessage API for businesses and AI agents. ZeroSettle joined Y Combinator’s Winter 2026 batch.[1] [3] Gabe grew up in Arroyo Grande, California, built his first iPhone app in high school, studied computer science at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, and worked on Apple operating systems from 2021 to 2025.[2] [3] [6]

Early life 1999–2014

Gabe grew up in Arroyo Grande, a small farming town on California’s Central Coast. He credits his upbringing there with teaching him the value of hard work and resilience.[3] [4] He attended Arroyo Grande High School.[18]

The launch of the first-generation iPad in 2010 sparked Gabe’s curiosity about how apps were made. He began learning iOS development at age 11.[3] [15] His first technology book was iPad Application Development For Dummies, by Neal Goldstein and Tony Bove.[3] [16]

In his early portfolio, he listed surfing and tennis among his favorite things to do outside of software.[5]

Early adolescence and high school 2014–2017

The Bruh App

Gabe built his first app, Bruh, in high school. The idea was simple. Tap a friend’s name and their iPhone would say “Bruh!” It became by far the most successful of his early projects, reaching thousands of users. He recalls the experience of getting his first app into people’s hands as intoxicating. When he introduced a feature called Bae Mode, he taught himself how to implement in-app purchases.[3]

In January 2015 he published a walkthrough of how he built Bruh, covering its interface, push notifications, and the Parse backend that handled friendships and blocking. A preserved listing records the app in the App Store’s Social Networking category.[6] [12]

College 2017–2021

From 2017 to 2021, Gabe studied computer science at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo, where he made the President’s List and Dean’s List. He recalls often skipping class to work on personal projects. At Cal Poly he met Ryan Elliott, a computer science classmate who became a friend and later his ZeroSettle co-founder.[2] [3] [7]

Early Career at Apple 2021–2025

Apple was Gabe’s first job after college. From 2021 to 2025 he worked as a software engineer on Apple operating systems.[2] [8] Gabe says the work he’s proudest of is writing code that still runs around the clock on two billion Apple devices.[3]

Transit and Apple Pay

Gabe worked on Apple Pay and transit features, including the Wallet transit-card integration in Maps. He also worked on next-stop suggestions for multistop routing and diagnosed memory growth in Maps and its destination prediction service.[2] [3] Apple demonstrated in its iOS 16 keynote how riders could add transit cards to Wallet and reload them without leaving Maps.[19]

ZeroSettle and Y Combinator 2026–present

ZeroSettle is an iMessage API for businesses and AI agents. ZeroSettle Pro costs $49 per month for one dedicated iMessage number, with a REST API for sends and signed webhooks for replies.[14]

Gabe started ZeroSettle with his friend and Cal Poly computer science classmate Ryan Elliott.[3] Both had worked as software engineers at Apple. Gabe became CEO and Ryan became CTO. The company joined Y Combinator’s Winter 2026 batch.[1] [7] Gabe says ZeroSettle raised an unannounced seed round from Y Combinator and other investors.[3]

ZeroSettle launched with a direct billing SDK that let mobile apps offer web checkout for in-app purchases. The launch described tools for subscription management, switching existing subscribers to direct billing, and payment processing.[9]

Dedicated iMessage numbers for AI agents

Teams use the dedicated line for customer support, appointment reminders, and sign-in by text.[10]

Grok, Claude, and ChatGPT can get access to a dedicated ZeroSettle line through its hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server on supported plans. The tools available depend on the client and plan. ChatGPT Pro, for example, can read conversations through a custom connector.[13]

Developers can use the API and webhooks to connect their own AI agent. Teams can also use ZeroSettle’s hosted AI agent to answer inbound texts without running their own server.[14]

Angel investing Active in 2026

Alongside running ZeroSettle, Gabe is an active angel investor. His public profile is concentrated in technology and startup circles, where he is known for his early apps, his engineering work at Apple, and co-founding ZeroSettle.[3] [11]
